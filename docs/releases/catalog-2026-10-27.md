# Treatment catalogue and inactive booking protection

The catalogue now offers 15 PURE, 10 DEFINE, 15 BEYOND and 10 PRIVATE choices.
Hautaufhellung and G8 are inactive; historical references are retained. The
classic facial name and full-area BEYOND Titan treatment match the live catalogue.
PRIVATE product descriptions reflect the confirmed revised combinations.

Token creation, slot validation, token consumption and booking redirects now
require an active treatment. Previously issued tokens cannot bypass retirement.

Validation: existing Node suite plus `test/inactiveBookings.test.js`. The new
test covers inactive treatment token creation and previously issued token use
without writing bookings. Deployment uses the existing Render service.

Cryo Contour's component counts do not establish a new appointment count.
The existing appointment allowance remains pending operational clarification.
